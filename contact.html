About the Author

Ben Shaw is a Kenilworth-based author and family historian. What began as an attempt to solve the mystery of a 1595 Geneva "Breeches Bible" handed down through his family led to the discovery that one of his direct ancestors was Bryan Blundell: master mariner, twice Mayor of Liverpool, co-founder of the Blue Coat School, and slave trader. Years of historical and genealogical research culminated in his first book, The Slave Trader: The Story of Bryan Blundell, published by Pen & Sword History on 30 August 2026.

The research that followed took him from parish registers and wills to muster rolls, port books and the archives of the Blue Coat School itself, piecing together a life that ran from a cabin boy’s berth to the quarterdeck of an Atlantic trader, the mayor’s chair, and the founding of a charity school that still stands. He is drawn to the awkward corners of history: the ones where generosity and cruelty sit in the same pair of hands, and where the comfortable story a town tells about itself turns out to be only half of it.

By day Ben works in the NHS. The rest of his time goes on record offices, family trees, and long walks around Liverpool’s waterfront working out where the eighteenth-century town once stood — walks that became the In the Footsteps of Bryan Blundell tour. He speaks to local history societies, schools and reading groups about Blundell, the Blue Coat School and Liverpool’s part in the Atlantic slave trade, and is always glad to hear from readers, fellow researchers, and anyone who thinks they may share a branch of the family tree.
