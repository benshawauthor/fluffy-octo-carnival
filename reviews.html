Book Reviews

Early praise for The Slave Trader: The Story of Bryan Blundell.

This thoroughly researched narrative of eighteenth-century mercantile Liverpool and its dominance in transatlantic trade highlights the entanglement of philanthropy, religion, finance and the barbarity of the slave trade. It is made more convincing, its contradictions starker, by drawing on one man’s own written account of the period. Bryan Biggs, Director of Cultural Legacies at the Bluecoat, Liverpool
An amazing book, beautifully written. Bill Lindsay, author, and editor of Topmasts, the online magazine of the Society for Nautical Research
An immensely readable book that brings Bryan Blundell’s world vividly to life. Bill Ogle, Liverpool Maritime Society
